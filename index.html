Summer (June–August) fire weather from one climate-model chain (RCA4 driven by MPI-ESM-LR), 0.15° grid (about 15 km). It describes weather conditions, not ignition probability. Source: F. Taccaliti, UNIPD-TESAF (C3S fire-danger indicators, Giannakopoulos & Karali 2020).

Future Drought Class Controls

Scenario / period
About this layer
What it showsDecember SPEI-12-equivalent values representing projected climatic water balance conditions relative to the historical reference climate (1981–2010).
How to read the valuesValues indicate the standardized position of projected future climatic water balance relative to the historical reference distribution (1981–2010). Negative = drier, positive = wetter than reference.
Legend> −1.0 low drought hazard · −1.5 to ≤ −1.0 moderate · −2.0 to ≤ −1.5 high · < −2.0 extreme.
Source: Stefan Ebner (BFW). Scenario/period labelling for the 4 bands is inferred, not embedded in the source file — flagged for confirmation.
